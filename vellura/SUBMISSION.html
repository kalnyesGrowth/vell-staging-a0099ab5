#1 — Female facial hair

Vertical: what problem

Unwanted coarse facial hair on the lip, chin and jawline in women. Unsexy, appearance-driven,

chronic, and already monetised — which is your own filter, three for three.

The daily behaviour is the product opportunity. She is not looking for a cure. She is

managing something on a two-to-three day clock: tweezers in the bag, the car mirror because

bathroom light lies, checking her jaw before a meeting. That is a maintenance habit with a

subscription shape already built into it.

Market: proof people pay

They already pay, just not to us. Waxing and threading run $30–60 a session on a 2–4 week

cycle. Laser runs $200–400 a session across 6–8 sessions and **does not hold against

hormonal regrowth**, which is precisely the PCOS-adjacent population we are selling to.

Electrolysis is the only permanent answer and costs $1,500–4,500 over years.

So the money is being spent. It is being spent on methods that cannot slow growth, by

someone who has usually already paid for at least one of them and been disappointed.

That last part matters more than market size. Per your own read on telehealth buyers: most

are repeat category buyers, and the creative has to answer why this works when the thing

you already bought did not. Here that answer is unusually clean, and it is not a

positioning trick — every method she has tried acts on the hair, and none of them act on
