Claims on this screen

  • C1 LABEL — Eflornithine slows the rate of facial hair growth.
    FDA-approved labeling for eflornithine HCl cream 13.9% (Vaniqa, NDA 021145). Indication: reduction of unwanted facial hair in women.
  • C2 LABEL — If you stop, hair returns to the way it was within about eight weeks.
    Vaniqa labeling: condition returns to pretreatment state ~8 weeks after discontinuation.
  • C3 LABEL — Most people need about eight weeks of twice-daily use before they see a change.
    Vaniqa labeling: improvement observed within 8 weeks of twice-daily use.
  • C4 NEEDS-MD — Azelaic acid is included for its effect on the follicle environment.
    Azelaic acid is well established in dermatology. Its ROLE IN THIS COMBINATION is rationale, not proven outcome.
  • C5 NEEDS-MD — Topical metformin is included as part of the compounded protocol.
    Appears as a hirsutism compounding protocol (PCCA-style formulations). Human efficacy evidence for TOPICAL metformin in hirsutism is limited.
  • C8 PLACEHOLDER — A licensed clinician reviews your answers within 24 hours.
    Operational SLA owned by Rimo. Not yet confirmed.
  • C9 PLACEHOLDER — Pricing: $99/mo, $249/3mo, $449/6mo, $25 one-time medical review.
    Modelled in deliverable/01-catalog-economics.md against a $150 CAC. Depends on an actual pharmacy quote for the 3-in-1 cream, which the catalog does not price.
