Rules

  • 3-month is pre-selected in this build. CORRECTION: it is no longer the payback-positive-at-M0 option. At the quoted per-gram rate the 3-month clears by roughly $5 over its whole term, and the 6-month is the only tier that is profitable regardless of retention. Moving the pre-selection to 6-month is a one-token change, held until the 3-active quote lands.
  • Double supply is a checkbox, never a separate upsell screen.
  • PRICE IS PROVISIONAL, and this note is for the dev and review layer only, never for the customer frame. $119 / $289 / $499 is the owner's base case, not a signed number. It is pending the pharmacy quote on the 3-active hero and is modelled against a $150 CAC on a per-acquisition cohort basis rather than per survivor. The final number follows the price rule and the bands in deliverable/02-submission.md.
  • ES / EL PRECIO ES PROVISIONAL. $119 / $289 / $499 es el caso base del dueno, no un numero firmado. Esta pendiente de la cotizacion de la farmacia para el compuesto de 3 activos y esta modelado contra un CAC de $150 sobre una base de cohorte por adquisicion, no por sobreviviente. El numero final sigue la regla de precio y las bandas en deliverable/02-submission.md.

Claims on this screen

  • C9 PLACEHOLDER — Pricing: $119/mo, $289/3mo, $499/6mo, $25 one-time medical review.
    Modelled in deliverable/02-submission.md as a cohort model against a $150 CAC and a $50/month cost stack (20g product $35 at the FP71 rate of $1.75/g, shipping $8, platform $7). The 3-active hero SKU is still unquoted; FP71 eflornithine came back at $35/20g.
  • C16 NEEDS-MD — Laser targets follicles that have already activated, and in PCOS it can trigger regrowth in previously clear areas.
    Paradoxical hypertrichosis after laser is documented in the dermatology literature and appears in our research brief. Patient reports in the VOC bank describe the same pattern.
  • C17 VERIFIED — The same three actives sell elsewhere from $119 a month.
    Oana Health 'Hairless Hype' (eflornithine 12% + metformin 5% + azelaic acid). Their hirsutism product page lists $119/month, and a 60g size at $139, plus a $20 one-time medical approval fee. The earlier $115 figure was the homepage 'from' price; $119 is the like-for-like monthly. Re-checked 2026-08-17.
  • C20 RESEARCH — Waxing runs $30-60 a session every 2-4 weeks. Laser runs $200-400 a session across 6-8 sessions.
    foundational-docs/hirsutism/04-competitive-landscape.md, non-telehealth alternatives table.
