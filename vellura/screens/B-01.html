Why it keeps coming back

Your body sends three signals to grow that hair.

An enzyme signal, a hormone signal, and an insulin signal.C19 Waxing removes the result of all three. Everything sold to you so far blocks one, at most.

Waxing and threading pull the hair out. Shaving cuts it at the surface. All three leave the follicle exactly as they found it, which is why the clock restarts the moment you finish. Laser targets follicles that already activated, and in PCOS it can trigger regrowth in areas that were clear.C16

Vellura Triple is applied to the skin, not to the hair, and it is built to answer all three signals at once. Its lead ingredient is eflornithine, which is used to slow the rate of facial hair growthC1.

Questions
I have tried everything and nothing works.

Everything you have tried targets the hair after it grew. This targets the three signals telling it to grow in the first place. It is a different problem, so it needs a different tool. That is also why it is slow: you are changing what the follicle does, not clearing what it already made.

What if it does not work for me?

Use it as directed for 90 days. If you see no change at all, message the care team and we refund a month: $119 monthly, $96.33 on the 3-month, $83.17 on the 6-month. There are no daily check-ins you can miss and no fine print that voids it.

Is this the same as Vaniqa?

No. Vaniqa is a single-ingredient FDA-approved product. Vellura Triple is a compounded cream with three actives, prepared for you by a licensed pharmacy, and it is not FDA-approved.

Does it work on the whole face?

It is applied to the areas you and your clinician agree on. 20g covers the upper lip and chin for about a month. If you need a larger area, there is a double supply option at checkout.
